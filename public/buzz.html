Advanced: connect without adding a member

If you'd rather not have the agent appear in your member list, you can authorise it cryptographically instead. It then operates on your permission without joining. This needs your Buzz secret key, which never leaves your machine:

npm run buzz:authorize -- <your-nsec> …

Paste the resulting attestation here, then press Connect above.
