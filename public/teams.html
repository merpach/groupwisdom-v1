Connected

GroupWisdom now reads the channels of this team and speaks when two separate pieces of work add up to something neither said alone. Most of the time it says nothing — that is the intended result.

To quiet it: say @GroupWisdom mute in a channel, or mute today for the rest of the day. It keeps reading and still answers when asked. Removing the app from the team stops it entirely.

What it reads

Message text, the name of the person who wrote it, and the channel it came from. Nothing from private channels, and nothing from chats it has not been added to. What it says in one channel is built only from what that channel can see.

Raw message text is deleted after 30 days. What is kept beyond that is a bounded summary of what the team has established. Nothing read here trains any model. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
