Add GroupWisdom to Slack

It reads only the channels you invite it to, each as its own project, and speaks when two pieces of finished work in a channel add up to something neither said alone. Most of the time it says nothing.

1
Sign in

The workspace is connected to your GroupWisdom account, and its findings live there.

Sign in on your account page, then come back here.

2
Agree to what it keeps, then add it
  • It reads messages only in public channels someone invites it to. Never private channels, direct messages, or channels shared with another company.
  • Messages are stored encrypted. The raw text is deleted after 30 days; what it has established about the channel is kept until you disconnect.
  • Messages are analysed by Anthropic's Claude, which does not train on them. Nothing from your workspace is used for anyone else.
  • Findings are written by an AI model and can be wrong. Each one quotes the message it rests on so it can be checked.
  • Disconnecting here, or removing the app in Slack, deletes everything it holds from the workspace.

3
Invite it to a channel

It reads nothing until you do. In the channel, send:

/invite @GroupWisdom

It says hello, explains what it keeps, and starts reading from that moment. Each channel you invite it to becomes its own project with its own memory, so what it learns in one channel never shapes what it says in another.

In the channel

A finding arrives as a reply to the message that completed it, and is also shown in the channel. Under each one are three buttons, Helpful, Wrong and Late. One press tells us whether it was worth saying, which is the only honest measure of whether this works.

/groupwisdom memory
What it has picked up in this channel, with the message each fact came from. Only you see the answer.
/groupwisdom mute
Quiet until someone unmutes it. mute today lifts at midnight UTC. It keeps reading.
/groupwisdom unmute
Back on.
/groupwisdom demo
A one-minute example on made-up messages, so the channel can see what it does. None of it is kept.

The same words work as a mention, @GroupWisdom memory, for anyone who prefers that.

What it reads

Message text, the name of the person who wrote it, and the channel, from the public channels it has been invited to. It does not read private channels, direct messages, files, or channels shared with people outside your workspace, and it never asks Slack for a channel's history: it starts from the moment it is invited.

Findings are written by an AI model and can be wrong, incomplete or late. Each finding records the words it rests on, and /groupwisdom memory shows what it believes, so a finding can always be checked rather than trusted. Nothing read here trains any model. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
